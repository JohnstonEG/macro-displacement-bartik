Research Highlights — Cyclical Demand Shocks and Aggregate Task Composition: Evidence from a Bartik Instrument

* Bartik LP-IV estimates how cyclical demand shocks reshape aggregate task content
* A one-point rise in unemployment cuts routine task content of the employed
* The effect persists over five years rather than reverting to baseline
* Routine-manual jobs fall while non-routine manual employment rises
* The adjustment runs within industries, not through industry mix
